Quick guide

How to use this map

1. Find a person

Search either the original or baptismal name. You can also search a place, year, or papal period.

2. Narrow the dates

Move the two handles on the single year timeline to focus on a historical period.

3. Choose a Papal Period

Use the dropdown to show one papal period, or keep All Papal Periods selected.

4. Read the evidence

Click a map point to narrow the list, then choose a person to open the six-field historical record.

Remember: the map shows reviewed origins only. Unresolved origins and missing origins remain searchable; Rome is not a destination marker.

Interpretive caution: points represent reviewed references for documented origin labels. They do not prove birthplace, route, captivity location, or destination. Generalized regional points are approximate.

All 781 people remain searchable. Original Date cells and inherited Years remain available in the complete CSV. Papal headings govern subsequent records; the Clement VIII assignment is explicitly marked as inferred.
